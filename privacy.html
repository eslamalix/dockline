Dockline Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Dockline is a widget bar for Windows made by Eslam Ali. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: Dockline has no account, shows no ads and does not collect information about you.

Information that stays on your PC

Your layout, chosen widgets, theme and other settings are saved in a file on your own PC. The battery, CPU, memory, music and Night Light information that widgets show is read from Windows on your PC and is not sent anywhere.

Information that leaves your PC

  • Radio. When you search for or play a station in the Radio widget, the search words are sent to radio-browser.info, a free community directory of radio stations, and the station's audio is streamed from the station's own server. Those services see your IP address, as any website does. Dockline sends nothing else to them.
  • Purchases and updates. When you buy Dockline, the Microsoft Store handles the payment and the license. Dockline only asks the Store whether your copy is a trial or bought. The Microsoft Store also delivers updates. Microsoft's handling of that information is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

What Dockline does not do

  • It does not collect usage statistics, crash reports or personal information.
  • It does not sell or share any information with anyone.
  • It does not use advertising or tracking.

Children

Dockline does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
